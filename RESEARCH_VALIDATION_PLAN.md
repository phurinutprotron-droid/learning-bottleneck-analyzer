# LBA research validation plan

1. **Freeze the protocol and software.** Record engine, pack, rubric, graph and question versions. Prespecify grade bands, inclusion criteria, outcomes, collection windows, handling of absences and access boundaries. Keep demonstration records excluded. Obtain institutional and participant authorization before collecting research data.
2. **Build a human reference set.** Select held-out authentic responses across grades, difficulty and subject. Have two independent subject specialists label supporting, contradictory and missing evidence using an agreed rubric. Blind them to model output and intervention assignment. Record initial agreement, disagreements, adjudication and reasons to abstain.
3. **Validate diagnostic performance.** Prespecify labels and a unit of analysis. Report confusion matrices, per-label precision and recall, abstention coverage and errors by grade and subject. Group train/development/test partitions and uncertainty estimates by learner. Report denominators and missingness. Evaluate calibration only if a future model produces calibrated probabilities.
4. **Validate selection and graph claims.** Compare adaptive with matched fixed verification using the same item budget and comparable forms. Measure diagnostic resolution, disagreement with expert labels, learner burden and unanswered items. Have experts independently rate graph edges and classify evidence as expert proposed, observational or experimental. Use a suitable controlled design for causal dependency claims.
5. **Evaluate learning outcomes separately.** Prespecify comparable baseline, post, transfer and delayed measures. Define direct, near and far transfer by task demands and expert review. Use controlled allocation where feasible, track implementation fidelity and account for learners nested within classes. Report paired or clustered estimates with uncertainty, attrition and form differences. Treat retention as a delayed outcome.
6. **Evaluate usability and AI boundaries.** Observe representative student and teacher tasks and collect task completion, errors and burden. Once live Gemini generation works, compare advisory outputs with independently reviewed references for unsupported citations, inappropriate claims, prompt injection and failure handling. For future OCR, retain original image, uncertain tokens, corrected transcription, teacher corrections and deterministic checks when available.
7. **Expand coverage deliberately.** Review mathematics and science packs and the Python tracing module first. Thai Language, Social Studies/Religion/Culture, Health and Physical Education, Arts, Career-related learning and Foreign Languages require their own reviewed packs. Coding currently covers variables and loops. Add other topics and formats after expert review and verify resource-limited isolation before any untrusted code execution.

Set acceptance thresholds, sample sizes and stopping criteria before recruitment with the research team. Publish protocol deviations and limitations. The local test suite is software QA evidence and contributes no research participants or efficacy observations.
