# Proposed collection protocol

1. Obtain reviewed content, ethics/school clearance, appropriate participant/guardian authorization, and a retention/deletion schedule.
2. Provision scoped staff and anonymous participant codes in an isolated deployment; never reuse real identities as handles.
3. Record pack, graph, engine and protocol versions. Freeze baseline-only comparison rules.
4. Collect initial item answers, reasoning and confidence without coaching or interim correctness feedback.
5. Have blinded reviewers judge evidence using a prespecified rubric. Maintain a controlled reference dataset outside application exports.
6. Randomize adaptive versus fixed matched verification selection externally; preserve allocations and comparable testing burden.
7. Collect verification and record supported, contradicted and inconclusive updates, including uninformative cases.
8. Teacher reviews evidence and selects/overrides an activity. Record the expected outcome and actual completion.
9. Collect independent post, held-out transfer and scheduled retention tasks. Record absent follow-ups rather than treating them as zero.
10. Collect a reviewed usability instrument (e.g. standard SUS with its original administration instructions). The API accepts the ten ratings; the UI does not claim ratings were collected.
11. Analyze paired/clustered data using a preregistered external analysis pipeline; report missingness, uncertainty and limitations.

Comparison scripts and real study allocations are intentionally not fabricated. The application does not yet implement random assignment or reviewer adjudication. Do not describe its heuristic selector as an experimentally validated adaptive test.
