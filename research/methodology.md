# Pilot methodology proposal — not executed

Research question: does evidence-driven verification improve agreement and revision quality compared with the same diagnostic rules without adaptive verification?

Recruit a feasible convenience pilot of approximately 30–50 consenting learners from a reviewed grade cohort only after ethics/school authorization and pack review. This is a planning range, not a power justification or a claim of recruited participants. Ask a statistician to size a confirmatory study using pilot variance and clustering. Include mathematics and science only when their respective content is approved. Report exclusions, missingness, language, age range and accessibility needs.

Two independent qualified reviewers judge deidentified response bundles against a prespecified rubric, record confidence and uncertainty, resolve disagreements through adjudication, and remain blinded to algorithm output and condition. Record initial agreement, adjudicated reference labels and reviewer rationale. Expert agreement is not ground truth about a learner's mind.

For diagnostic comparison, compute a frozen baseline-only result and a verification-augmented result for each participant. To separate the value of more questions from adaptivity, randomize verification selection to adaptive versus fixed matched-content items, using similar item counts and testing time. Preserve item versions, randomization assignments, protocol deviations and exclusions. A paired baseline/augmented comparison alone cannot identify the causal effect of adaptivity.

Primary outcome: change in expert-reference agreement, with participant-level paired analysis and uncertainty intervals appropriate to clustering. Secondary outcomes: revision/contradiction frequency, correctly revised versus newly incorrect reference judgments, calibration if a calibrated model is later established, testing burden, teacher usability and exploratory reliability. Heuristic support values must never be analyzed as calibrated probabilities.

Intervention learning outcomes form a separate exploratory component. Measure targeted pre/post performance, held-out transfer, and retention after a predefined interval (minimum seven days in this implementation). If learning effectiveness is studied causally, randomize reviewed interventions against an appropriate comparison, account for classroom effects, pre-register the estimand and plan, and avoid depriving learners of normal support. Different uncalibrated forms require equating or careful item-level analysis.

This application currently supports software data collection, descriptive metrics, aggregate export and external-label metric calculation. Recruitment, reviewer workflow, randomization, blinding, statistical inference, and research governance are not automated. No study has been run.
