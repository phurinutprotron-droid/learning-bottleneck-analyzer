# Metrics and interpretation

Correctness = count of approved-key matches / number of submitted items. Rubric alignment = lexical coverage; not independently rated reasoning quality. Evidence strength, uncertainty, support and priority are engineering heuristics.

Pre, post, transfer and retention proportions retain separate denominators and item versions. The descriptive difference is follow-up proportion minus the intervention's selected concept baseline proportion. Sample forms differ and are uncalibrated; do not interpret this as an equated learning gain or treatment effect. Retention includes elapsed days and is gated at seven days.

Reference evaluation computes paired raw agreement and Cohen's kappa; kappa is undefined if expected agreement is one. This measures agreement with the provided reference, not objective diagnostic truth. Verification summaries count evaluations with at least one contradictory hypothesis observation; this is not accuracy or proof of improved decisions.

Cronbach alpha uses complete binary baseline item matrices grouped by exact subject version; samples with fewer than two people, fewer than two items, or zero total-score variance return undefined. Alpha alone does not establish validity or unidimensionality. Different subject forms must not be pooled.

SUS calculation: sum odd-item (rating−1) and even-item (5−rating), then multiply by 2.5. It is a 0–100 usability score, not a percentage correct. Input validation requires ten integers from one to five. No SUS data is seeded. For confirmatory work use established administration, include sample uncertainty and external reviewed analysis.

Means, counts, and sample standard error are descriptive. Repeated measurements are not independent participants. No p-values, fabricated accuracy or causal conclusions are generated. Missing metrics return null and display an em dash.
