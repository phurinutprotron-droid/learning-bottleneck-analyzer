# Proposed hypotheses

- H1: Verification-augmented decisions have higher agreement with independently adjudicated expert reference labels than baseline-only decisions on the reviewed pilot items.
- H2: Adaptive verification yields more useful reference-supported revisions per verification item than fixed matched-content verification.
- H3: Teacher-reviewed targeted activities are followed by improved performance on reviewed follow-up tasks; without a randomized comparison this is descriptive, not causal.

Independent variables: diagnostic condition, verification selection condition, and (only in a separately approved experiment) intervention allocation. Dependent variables: reference agreement and Cohen's kappa, useful-revision rate, burden, post performance, transfer, retention and usability. Potential confounders include prior knowledge, item difficulty, reading proficiency, language, teaching exposure, item reuse, motivation and class context. Pre-register hypotheses, outcomes and exclusions before observing study data.
