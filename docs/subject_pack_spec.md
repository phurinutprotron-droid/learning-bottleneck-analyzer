# Subject pack specification

Subclassing is unnecessary: implement the Pydantic SubjectPack contract, then register an instance in PACKS. This does not require changing the diagnostic engine. Include subject ID, title, grade levels, version, graph version, review status, concepts/topics/skills, relationships, and items. Each item contains stage, kind, key aliases, objective, difficulty, concept, skills and prerequisites, rubric phrase groups and misconception terms. Stages are pre, verification, post, transfer, and retention.

Every relationship includes type, source, evidence status, confidence, version, and reviewer status. Prerequisite graphs must be acyclic. Pack validation rejects duplicate IDs and unknown item/relationship references. Relationship evidence categories distinguish author-defined samples, expert-defined content, statistical association, and experimental evidence. Never promote a category without a documented source and review.

The two initial packs contain three concepts and 21 items each: two baseline, two verification, one post, one transfer, and one retention item per concept. Mathematics covers fractions, proportional reasoning, and one-step equations. Science covers particles, thermal transfer, and energy conservation. The sample prerequisite chains are author-defined and unreviewed. They are content examples, not established causal models.

Before release for learner use: review curricular alignment and grade suitability, misconceptions and distractors, answer aliases, reasoning semantics, accessibility and language, prerequisite claims, transfer relevance, and item difficulty. Conduct cognitive interviews and pilot item analysis. Keep reviewer identity/decision evidence outside student data. Increment item/pack/graph versions for changes; historical assessment snapshots must remain reproducible. AI candidates can be reviewed and manually incorporated into a new pack, but never enter assessment delivery automatically.

Cross-subject links use fully qualified concept IDs in CROSS_SUBJECT_RELATIONSHIPS and are validated against the complete registry. GET /api/graph exposes the multi-pack graph with provenance. Its single illustrative ratios-to-energy-accounting connection is author-defined and unreviewed; it is not a prerequisite and is excluded from prerequisite traversal and diagnostic scoring. Reviewed cross-subject dependencies remain content-validation work.
